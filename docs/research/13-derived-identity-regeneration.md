# 13 — Derived entities that carry identity: regeneration determinism and stable keys

Project: **Skyline Together** (clean-room, host-authoritative co-op mod for Cities: Skylines II).

Conventions
- Paths are relative to `<audit>\decomp`. `file.cs:N` is line N of that file.
- **[V]** = verified by reading the decompiled code at the cited lines. **[I]** = inference or design reasoning.
- "Client" = any non-host peer. "Regenerate" = a vanilla Modification-phase system rebuilds the entity from its owner.
- Builds on reports 02 (tool pipeline), 03 (simulation), 04 (entities) and 09 (third-party mods).

## 0. TL;DR

1. **Lanes.** `LaneSystem` reuses lane entities through `LaneKey = (Lane{3×PathNode}, lane prefab Entity, Master|Slave flags)`
   (`Game/Game.Net/LaneSystem.cs:32-67`). [V] Edge lanes and building sub-lanes get keys that depend only on prefab and
   composition data. [V] **Node (intersection) lanes do not.** Their middle `PathNode` is a running counter (`nodeLaneIndex++`), and the
   generation order is partly sorted by **`Entity.Index`** (`SourcePositionComparer`, `:188-195`). Two-way lane direction is
   also decided by comparing owner entity indices (`:7137-7168`, `:7237`). [V] So node-lane keys, lane order and lane
   `PseudoRandomSeed` are **not guaranteed identical across peers**, even when the owner data is identical. [I, strongly
   supported] Lane identity must be mapped by a **portable key plus a geometric fallback**. Better still, keep lane references
   off the wire wherever possible (§2.5).
2. **Sub-objects and secondary objects** draw randomness from the owner's `PseudoRandomSeed` sub-streams
   (`kSubObject`, `kSecondaryObject`). [V] However:
   - placeholder choice depends on **renters, citizen ages, household wealth, pets, homelessness and snow**
     (`Game/Game.Objects/SubObjectSystem.cs:3121-3238`); [V]
   - node secondary objects consume randomness in `ConnectedEdge` buffer order (`Game/Game.Objects/SecondaryObjectSystem.cs:1274-1363`); [V]
   - tree sub-objects carry **simulated growth state** (`Tree`, with `Decoration` disabled), `SubObjectSystem.cs:2867-2869`, grown by
     `TreeGrowthSystem` (`Game/Game.Simulation/TreeGrowthSystem.cs:268-273`). [V]

   They are deterministic only if all of those inputs are replicated first. Their simulation state still has to be attached
   by key.
3. **Zone blocks.** Blocks are reused only on exact `Block` equality, per edge (`Game/Game.Zones/BlockSystem.cs:140-162, 978-1003`). [V]
   Vanilla keeps painted zoning across road edits through `CellCheckSystem` → `CellOccupyJobs.ZoneAndOccupyCellsJob`:
   - it copies `Cell.m_Zone` from **blocks deleted in the same frame**, nearest cell within √32 m (`Game/Game.Zones/CellOccupyJobs.cs:526-557, 611-643`); [V]
   - clients get this carry-over for free **only if** the old edge's deletion and the new edge's creation land in the
     **same frame**; [I]
   - host zoning must then be applied as an `m_Zone` overlay plus `Updated` on the block, after the client's blocks exist
     (§4.3).
4. **Definition-created children.** Building sub-nets, sub-areas, lots and area-spawned objects are never regenerated by a
   Modification system. [V, 02 §6] They come from `CreationFlags.Permanent` definitions whose seeds come from
   `RandomSeed.Next()`: `ZoneSpawnSystem.cs:1130,1177`, `BuildingConstructionSystem.cs:679`, `AreaSpawnSystem.cs:821`,
   `UpgradeDeletedSystem.cs:663`. [V] **They must be first-class replicated entities.** `UpgradeDeletedSystem` runs in
   `ToolUpdate` on every peer (`Game/Game.Common/SystemOrder.cs:711`). On clients it would re-create children that the host also
   replicates, so it must be disabled there. [I]
5. **Forwarding definitions (contradiction between 03 §9.3 and 02 §10) — verdict: do not forward definitions host→client.**
   Even `Permanent` definitions bring in:
   - nondeterministic randomness (`GenerateObjectsSystem.cs:979, 1417-1422, 1841`);
   - world-state reads: terrain and water height (`GenerateEdgesSystem.cs:1488`, `GenerateAreasSystem.cs:312`), the net
     search tree (`GenerateObjectsSystem.cs:603-682`), and connection to existing nodes (`GenerateNodesSystem.cs:1023`);
   - the per-machine `BuildOrder` counter (`GenerateEdgesSystem.cs:1630-1632, 1919-1943`);
   - per-entity random names (`RandomLocalizationInitializeSystem.cs:49, 123`);
   - machine-local identities that would still need post-hoc matching.

   **Replicate the resulting state instead** (a "spawn bundle" of entities with host NetIds). Let clients regenerate only
   identity-free or key-mappable derivatives. Definitions stay a **client→host intent** format only.
6. **Aggregates and names.**
   - The aggregate entity survives edge splits in the vanilla tool path, because the new edges inherit `Aggregated`
     (`GenerateEdgesSystem.cs:1027-1030, 1206-1208, 1687-1689`; `AggregateSystem.cs:79-95`). [V]
   - When aggregates merge, the survivor depends on hash-set iteration order (`AggregateSystem.cs:121-132, 402-462`). [V code / I effect]
   - Custom names are keyed by `Entity` (`Game/Game.UI/NameSystem.cs:162`). They are lost when their aggregate is deleted.
   - Generated street, building and citizen names come from `RandomLocalizationIndex`, which is seeded per machine. It must be replicated.
7. **Districts and road edges.**
   - `CurrentDistrict` and `BorderDistrict` are pure geometry functions computed by `CurrentDistrictSystem` (Mod5).
     **Recompute them on every peer.** [V]
   - `ServiceDistrict` is player-authored. Replicate it. [V]
   - `DistrictModifier` and `District.m_OptionMask` are pure functions of `Policy`. Replicate `Policy`, and replicate the
     results too, to avoid event double-application. [V/I]
   - `Building.m_RoadEdge` is recomputed by `RoadConnectionSystem` (Mod4B) on every peer. Hash-verify it and correct it by
     re-tagging, not by raw writes. [V/I]
8. **Routes.**
   - `RouteNumber` has no global counter. It is the smallest free number per prefab (`Game/Game.Routes/InitializeSystem.cs:55-104`).
     It is deterministic given the route set, but creations in the same frame collide.
   - `VehicleModel` uses `RandomSeed.Next()` (`:246`).
   - `Color` comes from the tool (`RoutePrefab.m_Color`, user-editable).

   Replicate all three, and write them **after** `Routes.InitializeSystem` (Mod4). Derive `AccessLane`, `RouteLane`,
   `PathTargets`, `PathElement` and `CurveElement` on clients. Replicate `Connected` explicitly: it is nulled when the stop
   is deleted and never re-matched (`WaypointConnectionSystem.cs:1136-1139`). [V]
9. **`NetCompositionData`** entities need no network identity. They are a per-prefab cache keyed by
   `(net prefab, CompositionFlags)` (`Game/Game.Net/CompositionSelectSystem.cs:1228-1265`) and are never saved (04 §4.1). [V]

## 1. Background facts used throughout

- `RandomSeed.Next()` draws from one static stream seeded with `DateTime.Now.Ticks` (`Game/Game.Common/RandomSeed.cs:8-17`).
  `GetRandom(i)` hashes the seed with `i` (`:20-24`). [V, 03 §3.1] Any value derived from `RandomSeed.Next()` therefore
  differs per machine.
- `PseudoRandomSeed{ushort}` is saved per entity and gives named sub-streams (`kSubLane`, `kSubObject`, `kSecondaryObject`,
  `kSplitEdge`, `kEdgeNodes`, `kDummyName`, …). [V, 04 §3] It is on the instance archetype of:
  - net geometry nodes and edges (`Game/Game.Prefabs/NetGeometryPrefab.cs:65, 83`);
  - all `ObjectGeometryPrefab` objects (`ObjectGeometryPrefab.cs:69`);
  - lots (`LotPrefab.cs:37`);
  - geometric lanes (`NetLaneGeometryPrefab.cs:52`);
  - marker objects, asset stamps and editor containers (grep). [V]

  **Not** on districts, surfaces, spaces or map tiles. [V by grep absence]
- `PathNode` packs `owner.Index << 32 | curvePos<<16 | segment<<8 | laneIndex` into a `ulong`
  (`Game/Game.Pathfind/PathNode.cs:20-40`). Equality, hashing and `GetOrder` all include the owner's **entity index** (`:62-80, 123-126`). [V]
- Lifecycle and phase facts are taken from report 02. Modification runs inside MainLoop after `ToolSystem`. Tags are cleared
  in the same frame's Cleanup. Remote mutations should be applied at MainLoop start (02 §9.1).

## 2. Lanes (Q1) — `Game/Game.Net/LaneSystem.cs`

### 2.1 Trigger, phase, reuse rule [V]
- Phase: `Modification4` (`Game/Game.Common/SystemOrder.cs:155`).
- Query: owners with `SubLane` and `Updated` or `Deleted`, excluding outside connections and `Area` owners (`:9259-9273`).
  Area lanes come from a different generator.
- Job inputs:
  - `m_RandomSeed = RandomSeed.Next()` (`:9373`);
  - `m_LeftHandTraffic` (`:9371`);
  - `m_DefaultTheme` (`:9374`);
  - terrain heights (`:9379`).
- **`LaneKey`** (`:32-67`) holds `Lane m_Lane` (the three `PathNode`s), `Entity m_Prefab` and `m_Flags & (Slave|Master)`.
  Equality compares all three (`:54-61`). The hash uses the `Lane` only (`:63-66`).
- `LaneBuffer` (`:231-263`) holds:
  - `m_OldLanes`: the current lanes of this owner;
  - `m_OriginalLanes`: the lanes of a Temp owner's original;
  - `m_SelectedSpawnables`: random state per randomization group;
  - `m_Updates`.
- `FillOldLaneBuffer` (`:2042-2152`) re-keys existing lanes before matching:
  - for edge lanes, the start and end nodes are replaced by the middle node with segment 0 or 4 (`:2101-2116`);
  - for node lanes, pedestrian and road end nodes are rewritten to match the adjacent edge lanes' middle nodes (`:2047-2083, 2118-2148`).
- Reuse rule, shown for an edge lane (`:4363-4474`):
  - an old lane with an equal key is reused unless its curve direction flipped (dot < 0, `:4376-4384`);
  - a reused lane gets new `Lane`, `EdgeLane`, `Curve` and type components, `Updated` (`:4386-4444`), and **a new
    `PseudoRandomSeed`** (`:4392-4401`);
  - otherwise a new entity is created from the prefab archetype (`:4475-4482`).

  The same pattern appears at `:3559`, `:3977`, `:4734`, `:7422`, `:7907`, `:8764`.
- `RemoveUnusedOldLanes` (`:2154-2169`) removes `Applied/Created/Updated` from unmatched lanes and adds `Deleted`.

### 2.2 Randomness [V]
- Edge, object and node owners use `nativeArray3[i].GetRandom(PseudoRandomSeed.kSubLane)`:
  - edges `:643`;
  - objects `:735`;
  - nodes `:842`.

  These arrays are read without a length check, so those owners must have `PseudoRandomSeed`. They always do, because it
  is in their archetypes.
- **Fallback:** `EditorContainer` owners without a seed use `m_RandomSeed.GetRandom(owner.Index)` (`:675`). This is
  editor-only and nondeterministic.
- The stream is consumed **sequentially in lane-creation order**. `CheckPrefab` (`:6712-6812`):
  - draws two `NextInt()`s per lane in every branch;
  - resolves placeholder lane prefabs by probability and by the city's `m_DefaultTheme` (`:6743`);
  - caches the chosen random state per randomization group in `m_SelectedSpawnables`;
  - returns `outRandom`, which seeds each new lane's `PseudoRandomSeed` (for example `:4370-4374`).

  So **lane prefab variants and lane seeds depend on the order in which lanes of one owner are generated.**

### 2.4 Determinism given identical owner data
Assume identical `Edge`, `Node`, `Curve`, `Composition`, `PseudoRandomSeed`, `Upgraded`, `BuildOrder`, theme, traffic side
and prefab set on two peers.

- **Edge lanes: deterministic** in set, order and keys.
  - Keys come only from the composition's lane list and the segment count, and both are pure functions of prefab and
    geometry. [V `:4147-4149`, §10]
  - The random stream is consumed in composition-lane order. [I]
- **Building sub-lanes: deterministic.** The prefab's `SubLane` buffer order is fixed (`:751-765`). [V]
  The curve heights use terrain (`NetUtils.AdjustPosition(..., m_TerrainHeightData)`, `:4639`), which affects only the curve,
  not the key. [V]
- **Edge connection lanes: deterministic given an identical `ConnectedNode` buffer order** (`:646-656` iterates it). [V]
  `ConnectedNode` is saved data (04 §4.1), so replicate the buffer **as an ordered list**. [I]
- **Node lanes: NOT guaranteed.** Offending inputs:
  1. **Connected-edge order.** `GetNodeConnectPositions` iterates connected edges through `EdgeIterator.AddSorted`, sorted
     by the `BuildOrder` midpoint (`:5124-5131`; `Game/Game.Net/EdgeIterator.cs:68-83`). [V] That is portable only if
     `BuildOrder` is replicated. `m_SortIndex = (start+end)>>1` can tie between different edges, and the
     `NativeArray.Sort` is unstable. Ties then fall back to `ConnectedEdge` buffer order, which can differ between peers
     (04 §5 caveat). [I]
  2. **`SourcePositionComparer` sorts by `m_Owner.Index` first** (`:188-195`). It is used for car (`:5698`), track
     (`:6561`), utility (`:7643`) and pedestrian/other (`:7966`) connect positions. `sourceBuffer[0]` then drives
     `SortTargets` (`:5699-5700`, `:6562-6563`). [V] For utility lanes, the main owner group is the first largest group in
     entity-index order (strict `>`, `:7648-7666`). [V]
  3. **Two-way lanes.** Two-way car lanes are created only when `source.Owner.Index < target.Owner.Index`, else `return false`
     (`:7163-7166`); likewise for two-way tracks (`:7237-7240`). The carriageway group depends on the same comparison
     (`:7137-7144`). [V] **The lane's direction (start and end nodes, curve orientation) therefore follows entity-index
     order.**
  4. **`LaneAnchor` sorting compares `m_Prefab.Index`** (prefab entity index) before order (`:225-228, 2511-2527`). [V]
     That is the same across peers only if prefab entities were created in the same order. It is machine-local in general
     (04 §1.2). [I]
  5. Because of 1–4, the **sequence of `nodeLaneIndex++`** (middle `PathNode` lane index), the **order of random draws**
     and therefore each lane's **placeholder prefab and `PseudoRandomSeed`** can differ. [I]
  6. `ConnectionKey` uses owner entity indices (`:69-90`). It is only a local dedupe set, so it does not affect keys. [V/I]
- **Cross-load stability.** `PathNode` stores the owner **entity index**. When the host saves and a client loads, entity
  indices are renumbered and `PathNode`s are re-encoded through the entity table (`PathNode.cs:133-149`, 04 §2.4). [V]
  Existing lanes stay consistent with their owners. [I] Every later regeneration re-keys from the client's own indices,
  and `FillOldLaneBuffer` re-derives keys from current lanes. So lane keys are internally consistent on each machine but
  are **never comparable across machines without re-encoding.** [I]

**Verdict [I]:** the generated lane *set* (topology) converges in practice for normal roads. Lane *identity* (key,
direction, seed, prefab variant) is reliable across peers only for edge lanes, edge-connection lanes (with ordered
`ConnectedNode`) and object sub-lanes. Node lanes need a geometric fallback.

### 2.5 Portable lane key and host-NetId → client-lane mapping [I]
- **Avoid lane references on the wire whenever possible.**
  - Vehicles and creatures should be replicated as transforms and frames (03 §9.5 option 1). Clients then need no
    `CarCurrentLane`.
  - Route lanes (`RouteLane`, `AccessLane`) and segment paths are re-derived locally (§9).
  - Lane references remain needed for parked cars (`ParkedCar.m_Lane`), lane-state UI, and any optional lane-based mover.
- **Portable lane key (PLK)** = `(ownerNetId, lanePrefabID, masterSlaveFlags, P(start), P(middle), P(end))`, where
  `P(pathNode) = (NetId of the entity whose index is in the high 32 bits, low 32 bits unchanged)`.
  - The low 32 bits carry lane index, segment, curve position and the secondary bit (`PathNode.cs:22-55`).
  - The host computes PLKs by mapping `PathNode.GetOwnerIndex()` (`:113-116`) back through its own index→NetId table.
  - The client resolves a PLK by building PLKs for the owner's current `SubLane` set in the same way.
- **Class-specific stability.**
  - For edge, edge-connection and object lanes, PLK equality is expected to be exact.
  - For node lanes, match by `(ownerNetId, lanePrefabID, flags, P(start).ownerNetId+lane/segment, P(end).ownerNetId+lane/segment)`
    and ignore the middle counter. If several lanes still match, or none, fall back to geometry: nearest
    `Curve.m_Bezier.a/.d` within ε, same prefab and same direction. If even geometry differs (a two-way lane created in the
    opposite direction), accept a **reversed match** and flip curve positions (`1-t`) when translating.
- **Mapping table.**
  - Keep `Dictionary<(ownerNetId, PLK), Entity>` per client, filled lazily when a message references a lane.
  - Invalidate all entries of an owner when the owner gets `Updated`/`Deleted` (the frame `LaneSystem` runs) or when the
    lane gets `Deleted`.
  - Lanes do **not** keep identity across an owner replace. `ApplyNetSystem` deletes and recreates lanes whose owner
    Temp has `Replace|Combine` (`Game/Game.Tools/ApplyNetSystem.cs:494-513`), and edge splits always replace edges
    (02 §4.3). [V] The table must therefore be rebuilt after every owner replace on both sides.
- **Host side.** Send PLKs, never lane entities. The host may also send the lane's quantized endpoints once per
  (owner, lane) to support the geometric fallback.
- **Traffic mod.** Report 09 §3 [W] says the Traffic mod disables vanilla `LaneSystem` and runs `TrafficLaneSystem`
  (Modification4). It also stores `GeneratedConnection`/`ModifiedLaneConnections` with raw `Entity` references. With
  Traffic installed:
  - every statement in §2.1–2.4 must be re-verified against its system (no local source is available);
  - its connection data must be replicated with entity remapping (09 P11);
  - the mod set must be identical on all peers.

  The PLK plus geometric-fallback design does not depend on vanilla internals, so it survives the replacement. [I]

## 3. `SubObjectSystem`, `SecondaryObjectSystem`, `SecondaryLaneSystem` (Q2)

### 3.1 `Game.Objects.SubObjectSystem` (Mod2B, `SystemOrder.cs:131`) [V]
- Trigger:
  - `SubObject` owners with `Updated`/`Deleted`; **or**
  - `Event` + `RentersUpdated`/`SubObjectsUpdated` (`Game/Game.Objects/SubObjectSystem.cs:3944-3960`).

  Producers of these events:
  - `RentersUpdated`: `PropertyProcessingSystem`, `PropertyRenterSystem`, `PropertyRenterRemoveSystem`,
    `HouseholdMoveAwaySystem`, `CompanyMoveAwaySystem`, `HouseholdAndCitizenRemoveSystem` (grep);
  - `SubObjectsUpdated`: `WetnessSystem` when the snow requirement flag flips (`Game/Game.Simulation/WetnessSystem.cs:87-94`)
    and `HumanMoveSystem`.
- Random source (`:1009`): the owner's `PseudoRandomSeed.kSubObject`, **else `m_RandomSeed.GetRandom(index)`** with
  `m_RandomSeed = RandomSeed.Next()` (`:4149`).
  - Deep sub-objects (sub-objects of sub-objects, up to depth 7) use their own seed (`:1109`). That seed exists only if the
    sub-object prefab has `ObjectGeometryData` (`m_HasRandomSeed = flag`, `:2492-2495, 2805-2806`). Otherwise the
    nondeterministic fallback `m_RandomSeed.GetRandom(index + num*137209)` is used. [V]
  - Upgrade-owned deep owners take the upgrade's seed but reuse the top owner's stream (`:1059-1069, 1111-1114`).
- **Placeholder requirements read simulation state** (`EnsurePlaceholderRequirements`, `:3121-3238`). They include:
  - `CityServiceUpkeep` resources;
  - storage company resources;
  - garbage facility;
  - each renter's company prefab and `CompanyData.m_Brand`, plus affiliated brands drawn from the random stream (`:3170-3184`);
  - household citizens' ages (`Children`/`Teens`, `:3192-3206`);
  - household wealth from `m_Income` and `PropertyRenter.m_Rent` (`:3207-3215`);
  - pets (`:3216-3219`);
  - homeless status (`:3220-3227`);
  - `Surface.m_AccumulatedSnow >= 15` (`:3235-3238`).
- Reuse key (`FindOldSubObject`, `:3322-3367`): same owner and same prefab (`FillOldSubObjectsBuffer`, `:1172-1196`), then
  either `Aligned.m_SubObjectIndex == alignIndex` or the **nearest old position**. Ties go to the first in multi-hash
  iteration order.
  - A reused sub-object **keeps its existing `PseudoRandomSeed`**. A new one is added only if missing (`:2630-2642`).
  - New sub-objects take `new PseudoRandomSeed(ref random)` (`:2492-2495, 2882-2890`).
  - The stream is advanced for every candidate, reused or not (`:2495`).
- Sub-object **simulation state**:
  - tree sub-objects of enabled prefabs get `Decoration` **disabled** (`:2867-2869`), which makes `TreeGrowthSystem` grow
    them (query `WithAllRW<Tree>`, `WithDisabled<Decoration>`, `TreeGrowthSystem.cs:268-273`);
  - `Destroyed`/`UnderConstruction` are mirrored from the owner on reuse (`:2695-2730`), so they are derived;
  - `StreetLight` state is recomputed from the top owner (`:2744-2770`);
  - `Stack` data (`:2771-2791`);
  - cranes' `PointOfInterest` is randomized by `BuildingConstructionSystem` (`BuildingConstructionSystem.cs:183-219`).

**Determinism verdict [I]:** deterministic **iff**:
- the owner has `PseudoRandomSeed`;
- the deep sub-object prefabs have geometry;
- every placeholder input (renters, company brand, household composition and wealth, pets, homelessness, snow) is identical
  at the moment the owner is regenerated;
- the previous sub-object set was identical (seed persistence on reuse).

Clients that do not simulate never receive `RentersUpdated`. The client apply layer must therefore **tag the building
`Updated`, or emit a `RentersUpdated` event, when it replicates renter changes**. Otherwise sub-objects go stale.
Snow-driven `SubObjectsUpdated` from a client-local `WetnessSystem` (03 §9.2 runs it as cosmetic) can create divergent
sub-object sets. `Surface` snow must be host-driven, or `WetnessSystem` disabled on clients. [I]

**State to replicate per sub-object, keyed (§11):** `Tree{m_State,m_Growth}` and the `Decoration` enable bit for trees.
Everything else on prefab sub-objects is derived. [I]

### 3.2 `Game.Objects.SecondaryObjectSystem` (Mod4B, `SystemOrder.cs:183`) [V]
- Trigger:
  - `SubObject` owners (not `Building`) with `Updated`/`ChangedDistrict`/`Deleted`;
  - district/policy `Modify` events;
  - updated owned `UtilityLane`s (`Game/Game.Objects/SecondaryObjectSystem.cs:4073-4095`).
- Random: owner `PseudoRandomSeed.kSecondaryObject`, else `m_RandomSeed.GetRandom(index)` with `RandomSeed.Next()`
  (`:1120, 4293`).
- Each secondary object's seed and random rotation come from `random.NextUInt` (`:3392-3403`). Secondary trees get a random
  age (`:3499-3504, 3619`).
- **Node** objects iterate connected edges through an unsorted `EdgeIterator` (raw `ConnectedEdge` order) and draw
  `random.NextFloat` per object (`:1274-1363`). [V] Draw order therefore follows a reverse buffer whose order can differ
  between peers. [I]
- **Edge** objects iterate the composition's `NetCompositionObject` list, which is deterministic (`:2528-2546`). They depend
  on `BorderDistrict` plus the district option `ForbidBicycles` (`:2539-2542`).
- **Area** objects (lots) are placed at each area node (`:2972-3006`).
- It also writes `Road.m_Flags` `IsLit`/`AlwaysLit` (`:1135-1154`), which is therefore **derived**, and traffic-light pole
  states (`:3488-3497`).
- Owners lacking `PseudoRandomSeed`: any `SubObject` owner whose archetype lacks it. In game mode all net-geometry, object
  and lot owners have it (§1). Only editor containers and non-geometry prefabs fall back. [I]

**Verdict [I]:** deterministic for edges and lots. For nodes, deterministic only if the `ConnectedEdge` buffer order matches.
Secondary trees carry `Tree` growth (simulated) → key them as in §11. Traffic-light visual state is derived from node
`TrafficLights` and lane signals, so replicate those, not the objects.

### 3.3 `Game.Net.SecondaryLaneSystem` (Mod4B, `SystemOrder.cs:184`) [V]
- No random source (grep `Game/Game.Net/SecondaryLaneSystem.cs`: no `RandomSeed`).
- Key: `LaneKey(Lane, prefab)` (`:28-60`). Lanes get **sequential** `PathNode(owner, laneIndex++)` marked secondary (`:1345-1350`).
- Pairing and dedupe picks the creating lane by `laneCorner.m_Lane.Index > laneCorner2.m_Lane.Index` (`:639, 791`).
  Creation order and the counter therefore depend on primary-lane entity indices.
- `LaneSystem` ignores secondary lanes when re-keying (`LaneSystem.cs:2087-2090`).

**Verdict [I]:** not guaranteed identical. Nothing replicated refers to secondary lanes (they are visual: markings, wires,
fences), so **no identity is needed**.

## 4. Zone blocks (Q3)

### 4.1 Block regeneration and reuse key [V]
- `BlockSystem` (Mod4, `SystemOrder.cs:156`) query: non-Temp `Edge` + `SubBlock` with `Updated`/`Deleted`
  (`Game/Game.Zones/BlockSystem.cs:1124-1137`).
  - A deleted edge marks all its blocks `Deleted` (`:99-112`).
  - Otherwise `FillOldBlockBuffer` maps `Block → entity` (`:140-148`). The key is the whole `Block` struct

    `Game.Zones.BuildOrder` and `Updated` are set (`:978-985`).

### 4.2 How vanilla preserves painted zoning across a road rebuild [V]
- `CellCheckSystem` (Mod5, `SystemOrder.cs:215`) runs when zones, objects, nets, lots or map tiles were updated
  (`Game/Game.Zones/CellCheckSystem.cs:216-225`).
  - It snapshots **`Block + Deleted` (non-Temp) chunks** (`:198, 236`).
  - It passes them to `CellOccupyJobs.ZoneAndOccupyCellsJob` (`:279-298`).
- `ZoneAndOccupyCellsJob.Execute` (`Game/Game.Zones/CellOccupyJobs.cs:611-664`):
  1. `ClearOverrideStatus`: `Overridden` cells lose the flag and their zone (`:666-682`).
  2. **`DeletedBlockIterator` copies `m_Zone` from cells of blocks deleted this frame** (`:624-643`). A new cell receives
     the zone (`:526-557`) only if all of these hold:
     - it is not `Blocked`;
     - it has no zone yet;
     - the old cell is `Visible` and not `Shared`/`Overridden`;
     - the old cell has a zone;
     - the centre distance² is ≤ 32;
     - it is the nearest candidate.
  3. Objects occupy cells, then `SetOccupiedWithHeight`.

  Later jobs:
  - `BlockCellsJob` recomputes `Blocked`/`Visible`/road flags and `ValidArea` (`CellCheckSystem.cs:239-261`);
  - `CheckBlockOverlapJob` resolves overlaps using `Game.Zones.BuildOrder` (`:309-319`);
  - `ZoneToggleJob` applies `Upgraded` zoning toggles (`:299-308`);
  - `UpdateLotSizeJob` rebuilds `VacantLot` (`:326-340`).
- Consequences:
  - **Only `Cell.m_Zone` is authored.** `m_State` flags and `m_Height` are recomputed. [V]
  - The carry-over works only if the old blocks still exist with `Deleted` in the same frame in which the new blocks are
    examined. `BlockSystem` writes through `ModificationBarrier4`, which plays back before Mod5, so within one frame this
    holds. [V/I]
  - The player zone tool writes `m_Zone` only for `Selected` cells and tags the block `Updated` (`ApplyZonesSystem.cs:111-140, 104-107`).
    `Zones.UpdateCollectSystem` collects bounds of `Created/Updated/Deleted` blocks (`Game/Game.Zones/UpdateCollectSystem.cs:156-166`).
    That is the vanilla "apply zoning" signature. [V]

### 4.3 Block key and Cell apply order [I]
- **Block key:** `(ownerEdgeNetId, side, quantized Block.m_Position (1/64 m), m_Size)`, where `side = sign(dot(m_Direction, edge right))`.
  Match exactly first; fall back to nearest position within 1 m on the same side. Exact `Block` equality is expected
  across peers when edge source data is identical. Quantization guards against Burst float differences (03 N11).
- **Zone payload:** per block, an array of `m_Zone` translated to zone `PrefabID` (`ZoneType.m_Index` is machine-local,
  04 §4.5). Omit cells whose host state is `Overridden`, because those are derived from overlap.
- **Order on a client for a road edit:**
  1. **Frame N, MainLoop start:** apply the structural change atomically. That means old edge `Deleted`, new edges created
     and the closure tagged, **in the same frame**. The client's Mod4 `BlockSystem` then deletes and creates blocks, and
     Mod5 `CellCheckSystem` carries zoning over exactly as on the host.
  2. **Frame N+1 (or when blocks with matching keys exist), MainLoop start:** apply the host's per-block `m_Zone` overlay
     and add `Updated` to each touched block. CellCheck recomputes flags, overlap, `VacantLot` and occupancy in that
     frame's Mod5, which mirrors `ApplyZonesSystem`.
  3. Buffer zone payloads whose block key is not yet present. Retry each frame, and drop after a timeout plus a resync request.
- Never write `m_State`, `m_Height` or `VacantLot`. Hash `m_Zone` per block for anti-entropy.

### 6.2 Verdict on forwarding Permanent definitions (the ZoneSpawn pattern) [I]
**Not viable as the convergence mechanism for simulation-spawned buildings. Replicate the results.** Reasons:
1. **Identity.** The created building, nodes, edges, areas and sub-objects are still machine-local entities. Mapping them
   to host NetIds needs per-entity keys for every child. That is the same information a state bundle carries, plus
   fragile matching (exact-transform owner matching `FindOwnersSystem.cs:83`; split/connect results).
2. **Residual randomness.** `UnderConstruction.m_Speed` (`GenerateObjectsSystem.cs:1421`) and `RandomLocalizationIndex`
   (`RandomLocalizationInitializeSystem.cs:49`) would need correction anyway.
3. **World dependence.** Sub-net and sub-area heights (terrain, water), connections to existing nodes, and the per-machine
   `BuildOrder` counter make results depend on client state at replay time. Any divergence silently produces different
   geometry.
4. **Later events are not definition-driven.** Completion (`BuildingConstructionSystem` deletes and re-creates sub-areas
   and sub-nets), level-up, abandonment and upgrade deletion run in the simulation or in ToolUpdate. They would need their
   own forwarding, or must be disabled and replicated anyway.
5. **Bandwidth is not the issue.** A spawn bundle is a few hundred bytes (building plus at most tens of children).
   Buildings spawn a few times per minute (03 §6.3).

Permanent-definition forwarding is acceptable only for **identity-free, non-simulated, cosmetic content**. Nothing in the
replicated set qualifies. The contradiction between 03 §9.3 and 02 §10 is resolved in favour of **02 §10 / state
replication**, applied **also to simulation-originated spawns**. 03 open Q1 is answered: generation is **not**
deterministic given only the definition.

## 7. Aggregates and names (Q6)

### 7.1 Aggregate identity [V unless noted]
- `AggregateSystem` (Mod2B, `SystemOrder.cs:130`), job `UpdateAgregatesJob` (`Game/Game.Net/AggregateSystem.cs:65-193`):
  - **Created edges whose `Aggregated.m_Aggregate` is set** are appended to that aggregate (`:79-95`). Created edges
    without one go to `emptySet` (`:96-99`). Updated edges put their aggregate into `edgeSet` (`:103-119`).
  - `ValidateAggregate` (`:531-592`):
    - keeps the aggregate entity;
    - re-walks connectivity from the **first non-deleted element in the buffer** (`:536-555, 567-578`);
    - drops edges of another aggregate type;
    - sends edges not reached back to `emptySet` (`:580-591`);
    - deletes the aggregate if no element survives (`:557-564`).
  - `CombineAggregate` (`:402-462`) merges neighbouring aggregates whose end edge connects: **the aggregate being
    processed absorbs the other, which gets `Deleted`**. The processing order is `edgeSet.ToNativeArray()` (`:123-132`),
    which is hash-set order and depends on entity values. [V code; I: the survivor is not peer-stable]
  - `CreateAggregate`/`TryCombine` (`:195-310`) build new aggregates for `emptySet` edges, or append them to an adjacent
    aggregate. `emptySet` iteration is also hash order (`:137-147`). [V]
  - `GetBestConnectionEdge` iterates the `ConnectedEdge` buffer (`:330-381`). Exact score ties fall to buffer order. [V/I]
- **Splits (vanilla tool path):** a split edge's Temp copy takes `Aggregated` from the original
  (`GenerateEdgesSystem.cs:1027-1030, 1206-1208`, and `:1687-1689` for edited edges). On apply, `ApplyNetSystem` keeps it
  unless the aggregate is a Temp (`ApplyNetSystem.cs:433-439`). The new permanent edges are `Created` with the old
  aggregate and are appended (`AggregateSystem.cs:79-95`). The original is `Deleted` and pruned by `ValidateAggregate`.
  **The aggregate entity survives splits.** [V]
- **Merges** (an edge removed between two streets, a `Combine` edge, or two aggregates that become connected): one
  aggregate entity is deleted. Which one depends on processing order. [I]
- **Implication for replication [I]:**
  - Treat aggregates as **first-class entities with NetIds**: `Aggregate` + `PrefabRef` + ordered `AggregateElement`
    (edge NetIds) + `RandomLocalizationIndex`.
  - When creating or replacing edges on a client, write `Aggregated.m_Aggregate = mapped aggregate` **before Mod2B**, so
    `AggregateSystem` appends instead of inventing new aggregates.
  - Run a reconciliation system `UpdateAfter<AggregateSystem>(Modification2B)` on clients. It forces the host's membership
    and element order and undoes deletions of host-surviving aggregates by removing `Deleted` (`AggregateSystem` itself
    does this at `:178-182`). Do it in the same frame, before `PrepareCleanUpSystem` destroys them.
  - Element order matters for addresses (§7.3).

### 7.2 Custom street names [V]
- Storage: `Dictionary<Entity,string> m_Names` (`Game/Game.UI/NameSystem.cs:162`). `SetCustomName` resolves `Controller`,
  writes the dictionary, and adds or removes the tag `CustomName` plus `BatchesUpdated` through `EndFrameBarrier` (`:214-241`).

- **When the aggregate is rebuilt:** the name survives as long as the aggregate entity survives (splits). It is

    `Temp.m_Original`, creates inserted ones as new entities, and deletes removed ones (`Game/Game.Tools/ApplyRoutesSystem.cs:274-460`).
  - **Replication [I]:** run the tool only on the acting peer. The host broadcasts the resulting `Route` (+`TransportLine`,
    `Color`, `RouteNumber`, `VehicleModel`, `Policy`, `RouteModifier`, `HiddenRoute`), `Waypoint{m_Index}` + `Position` +
    `Connected` + `Owner`, and `Segment{m_Index}` + `Owner`, each with a NetId. Clients derive `AccessLane`, `RouteLane`,
    `PathTargets`, `PathElement`, `CurveElement`, `ConnectedRoute` and `SubRoute`.

## 10. `NetCompositionData` (Q9) [V]
- `CompositionSelectSystem.GetOrCreateComposition` returns a cached entity for `(net prefab, CompositionFlags)`, or creates
  one with `PrefabRef` + `NetCompositionData{m_Flags}` + pieces from prefab sections. It registers the entity in the
  prefab's `NetGeometryComposition` buffer (`Game/Game.Net/CompositionSelectSystem.cs:1228-1277`).
- `SerializerSystem` excludes these entities (04 §2.1).
- Edges reference them through `Composition` (empty-serializable, derived). `LaneSystem` reads lane layouts from them.
  Their contents are a pure function of the prefab and flags.
- **No network identity is needed.** If one is ever required (debugging), the key is `(net PrefabID, CompositionFlags)`.
  Replicate the inputs that select the flags instead: `Upgraded`, `Elevation`, topology.

## 12. Final verdict: definition forwarding versus state replication

1. **Host→client: state replication only.** Every entity that anything else refers to, or that carries simulation state,
   is first-class with a host NetId. That includes:
   - nets, including building sub-nets;
   - areas, including lots and sub-areas;
   - top-level and area-spawned objects;
   - routes, waypoints and segments;
   - aggregates.

   Each carries full source components (§5, §11). [I, supported by §2.4, §5, §6.1]
2. **Clients regenerate** lanes, prefab sub-objects, secondary objects and lanes, blocks, compositions, geometry,
   district assignments and road-connection data with the vanilla Modification systems. Where replicated state points
   into those families (lanes, stop sub-objects, tree growth, zone cells), use the **portable keys** in §11. Resolve them
   **one frame after** the structural change has been processed. Fall back to geometry where the key is not peer-stable
   (node lanes, node secondary objects).
3. **Initializer-owned fields** (`RandomLocalizationIndex`, `RouteNumber`, `VehicleModel`, `UnderConstruction.m_Speed`,
   `DistrictModifier` of created districts) are overwritten with host values **after** their initializer has run. That
   means a post-initializer patch system in the same frame, or the start of the next frame.
4. **Disable on clients:**
   - definition producers: `ZoneSpawnSystem`, `BuildingConstructionSystem`, `AreaSpawnSystem` (already GameSimulation, 03 §9.6);
   - `UpgradeDeletedSystem` (ToolUpdate);
   - sources of cosmetic-but-structural divergence (`WetnessSystem`'s `SubObjectsUpdated`, or host-driven snow).
5. **Definitions remain valid in one direction only:** client→host intents (02 §10.4). The host executes or validates
   them and re-broadcasts the resulting state. 03 §9.3's "forward definitions for player edits and zone spawns" is
   **withdrawn**.

## 13. Risks and open questions
- **Traffic mod** replaces `LaneSystem` (09 §3 [W]). §2 must be re-verified against its decompiled `TrafficLaneSystem`
  before relying on PLK exactness. The geometric fallback is designed to be robust to this.
- **Burst float determinism** (03 N11) may make "exact" keys (`Block` positions, PLK curve fallbacks) differ by ULPs across
  CPUs. Always quantize keys. [I]
- **Reverse-buffer order** (`ConnectedEdge`) leaks into node lanes, node secondary objects and aggregate tie-breaks. It
  cannot be replicated directly (rebuilt on load and maintained by insertion). Consider a client-side patch that sorts
  `ConnectedEdge` by (`BuildOrder`, NetId) after `ReferencesSystem` (Mod2B). That would make these families deterministic
  across peers. [I; needs a Harmony or system-order spike]
- **`NameSystem` not updated** (§7.2). Verify at runtime whether its `OnUpdate` ever runs (for example a breakpoint or
  log). It affects whether stale custom names linger.
- **Sub-object reuse keeps seeds** (`SubObjectSystem.cs:2637-2641`). A client that joined from a save and the host can
  diverge if their regeneration histories differ. Periodic per-owner hashing of the sub-object set (prefab + quantized
  position) is advisable.
- **Two-way node lanes created in opposite directions** change curve orientation. Every translated curve position must
  respect the reversed-match flag.
- Open: does `RoadConnectionSystem`'s utility connection lane creation (`RoadConnectionSystem.cs:1888-1938`) use
  randomness? None was seen in the reviewed lines; not fully traced.
- Open: exact `WaterSystem` influence on Permanent sub-net heights for shoreline or floating buildings (`GenerateEdgesSystem.cs:1488`).
  It is moot under state replication.
